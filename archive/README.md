# Archived projects (not published)

These case studies were on the old site and are kept here, unpublished, so they can
come back later in the Terracotta Rose case-study template (`src/data/case-studies.json`
+ `src/pages/work/[slug].astro`). Nothing in this folder is built or deployed.

Each `index.md` uses the old content schema. To bring one back, add an entry to
`src/data/case-studies.json` (and a card to `src/data/work-grid.json`), move its images
into `src/assets/work/<slug>/`, and only publish copy, results and images Ani has approved.

NZ CrossFit Nationals and the Sunseeker beer label were hidden drafts before the
redesign; the Sunseeker can now appears inside the Flavours of Plenty case study.
