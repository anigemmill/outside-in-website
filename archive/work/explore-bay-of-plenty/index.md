Led the development and launch of the Explore Bay of Plenty destination app, a free digital guide designed to help visitors and locals discover more of the region.

Working with Sean Buchanan and a wider team of developers and community partners, the project brought regional experiences, local deals, food destinations, activities and walking and cycling tracks into one digital destination.
