## 2. Your rights come first

**You have rights under New Zealand law, including the Consumer Guarantees Act 1993 and the Fair Trading Act 1986, that these Terms can't take away.** Nothing in these Terms limits them. Under the Consumer Guarantees Act, our services must be provided with reasonable care and skill, be fit for any purpose you told us about, be finished in a reasonable time, and cost a reasonable price if no price was agreed. If they aren't, you're entitled to a remedy, which can include having the problem fixed, a refund, or cancelling.

## 3. The service

Findable checks how AI assistants and search engines describe a business, and gives you reports and recommendations. To do that we send questions to AI and search providers, such as OpenAI, Perplexity and Google (through SerpApi), and record what comes back. Those providers are outside our control, and Findable isn't affiliated with or endorsed by any of them.

What your plan includes, and its price, is shown at [theoutsidein.nz/findable/pricing/](https://theoutsidein.nz/findable/pricing/) when you buy it. [[REQ-14]]

**AI answers can be wrong.** Our reports show what AI tools said at the time we asked. Those answers can be incomplete, out of date or inaccurate, and they change. We don't promise any search ranking, AI mention, traffic or bookings. A member of our staff checks every report before you see it. [[REQ-16]]

## 4. Price and paying

Prices are in New Zealand dollars. We aren't registered for GST, so no GST is charged. If we register, the price you pay won't go up without the notice in this clause. You pay in advance, by invoice. We'll tell you before we move to card payments.

**Renewal.** A monthly plan renews each month, and an annual plan each year, until you cancel. We'll email you at least 30 days before an annual plan renews. [[LAWYER: Commerce Commission guidance on subscription renewal]]

**Price changes.** We'll give you at least 30 days' notice by email of a price increase. You can cancel before it takes effect, at no cost.

## 5. Cancelling

You can cancel at any time by emailing anigemmill@theoutsidein.nz. Your plan stays on until the end of the period you've paid for, and isn't charged again.

**Refunds.** Your rights under the Consumer Guarantees Act apply (clause 2). On top of those, if we can't deliver an analysis you've paid for, we'll refund that month.

Keep your sign-in details safe, and tell us promptly if you think someone has got into your account. Before we run a real analysis we'll ask you to confirm which providers we may use. [[REQ-11]]

You may only ask us to analyse a business you own or have permission to act for. Please don't put sensitive information into Findable. See the [Acceptable Use Policy](05-acceptable-use-policy.md).
