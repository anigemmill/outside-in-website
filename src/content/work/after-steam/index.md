challenge: >-
  Geothermal attractions are built around the daytime visit. By evening the
  car park empties, while thousands of visitors staying in town are looking
  for something to do. The site, the stories and the staff are already there.
  What is missing is a reason to come back after dark.
approach: >-
  Treat the evening as its own product, with its own name, look and booking
  journey, rather than an add-on to the day ticket. Design it for people who
  are already in town, and use technology quietly: to guide, translate and
  answer questions, so staff can spend their time on hosting.
developed:
  - A sub-brand for the evening experience, built to sit alongside the parent brand without competing with it
  - A mobile visit companion (a web app, no download) with self-guided story points opened by QR
  - A multilingual question assistant trained only on approved venue content, with clear hand-off to staff
  - Pre-arrival and post-visit email journeys that suggest a return visit or a daytime add-on
  - Launch campaign aimed at visitors already staying in town
map:
  outside:
    - Evening brand, signage and wayfinding
    - Booking page and launch campaign
    - Visit companion on the visitor’s phone
  inside:
    - Approved knowledge base the assistant answers from
    - Booking data feeding the email journeys
    - Weekly report on questions asked and unanswered
objectives:
  - Grow evening visitation from visitors already in town
  - Increase the share of visitors who book a second experience
  - Reduce repeat questions at the front desk
seoDescription: Concept project. An after-dark visitor experience for a geothermal attraction, with a sub-brand, mobile visit companion and multilingual assistant.
---

This concept explores a question many attractions share: what happens to the
asset after closing time? The proposal keeps the cultural and natural story at
the centre and uses the evening's atmosphere as the point of difference.
