challenge: >-
  Marketing teams watch social trends, search demand, competitors and industry
  news across a dozen tabs. The useful signal arrives late, or it arrives in
  someone's head and never reaches the meeting where decisions get made.
approach: >-
  Let agents do the monitoring and first-pass summarising. Keep people in charge
  of judgement. Every item in the brief is tagged to the brand it affects and
  ends with a suggested action, so the brief is something to act on, not read
  and forget.
developed:
  - Monitoring agents for social trends, search demand, competitor activity and industry news
  - A one-page daily brief with each item tagged to the business it affects and a suggested next step
  - Delivery into the tools the team already uses, such as Notion, Slack or email
  - A simple rating loop so the team can mark items useful or noise, which tunes future briefs
  - Documentation so the team can adjust sources and topics without us
map:
  outside:
    - One consolidated visual brief each morning
    - Per-brand tags and suggested actions
    - Weekly roll-up for the marketing meeting
  inside:
    - Source lists and topic rules per brand
    - Agents that collect, de-duplicate and summarise
    - Feedback ratings that tune what gets included
objectives:
  - Cut the time spent on manual monitoring
  - Shorten the gap between a trend appearing and a decision about it
  - Start each marketing meeting from evidence
seoDescription: Concept project. An AI-powered daily marketing intelligence brief covering social, search and industry trends, tagged by brand with suggested actions.
---

The design principle here is restraint. A brief that tries to report everything
gets ignored by week two. Field Notes is built to say less, more usefully.
