challenge: >-
  Regional festivals have a short window to sell tickets and a shorter one to
  capture the weekend. Content is often planned the week before, shot on the
  day and published once the crowd has already gone home.
approach: >-
  Build the campaign and the content system together. One flexible visual idea
  stretches from the first poster to the last recap, and a production plan gets
  content from the field to the feed within hours, with AI-assisted clipping and
  captioning so a small crew can keep up.
developed:
  - Campaign idea and visual identity for the festival
  - A modular template kit for social, signage, screens and print
  - A content plan covering the run-up, the weekend and the recap
  - An on-the-day production workflow with AI-assisted clipping and captions
  - A post-event recap built to open early-bird sales for next year
map:
  outside:
    - Posters, social, signage and screens
    - Same-day highlights and artist moments
    - Recap and early-bird campaign
  inside:
    - Template kit and brand rules
    - Shot list, crew roles and publishing rota
    - Clipping and captioning workflow
objectives:
  - Sell through earlier in the campaign window
  - Publish weekend content within hours, not days
  - Leave a system the organisers can reuse next year
seoDescription: Concept project. A festival campaign and content system with a modular template kit and an AI-assisted on-the-day production workflow.
---

A festival is the rare brief where speed is part of the creative. The system is
designed so the best moments of the weekend are online while people are still
talking about them.
