challenge: >-
  A coastal accommodation business is full every summer weekend and quiet every
  winter week. Discounting has trained guests to wait for a deal, and most
  bookings arrive through travel sites that take a commission.
approach: >-
  Stop apologising for winter. Sell what only the off season offers, a quiet
  beach and an empty coast, to the audiences who can travel midweek: remote
  workers and couples looking for a short escape. Drive bookings to the direct
  site and test every assumption.
developed:
  - Audience research and two priority segments with clear motivations
  - Campaign line and creative built around having the coast to yourself
  - A direct-booking landing page designed around one action
  - A channel plan across social, search and email
  - A test plan with stated hypotheses, success measures and a weekly review
map:
  outside:
    - Campaign creative and landing page
    - Social, search and email activity
    - Offers built on experience, not discounts
  inside:
    - Audience segments and test hypotheses
    - Conversion tracking on the direct site
    - Weekly review and budget reallocation
objectives:
  - Lift midweek winter occupancy
  - Move a larger share of bookings to the direct site
  - Learn which message and audience convert, for next winter
seoDescription: Concept project. An objective-led digital marketing campaign to grow midweek winter bookings for a coastal accommodation business.
---

The campaign is designed as an experiment as much as an advert. Each creative
route has a hypothesis attached, so the results teach something either way.
