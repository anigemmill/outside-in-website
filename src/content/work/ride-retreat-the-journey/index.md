Designed a mobile-first digital concierge to bring the entire Ride Retreat experience together in one place, replacing a fragmented process of emails, forms and PDFs with a connected guest experience.

The experience brings together an interactive itinerary, trail information, packing lists, waivers, travel and pickup details, dietary requirements, fellow riders, important updates and retreat contact information. Additional features include a countdown, local recommendations, notifications and a dedicated space for retreat photography and video.
