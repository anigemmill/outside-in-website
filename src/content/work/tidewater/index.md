challenge: >-
  A science-led startup has to convince two very different audiences at once.
  Farmers want proof it works on their land. Investors want to see a business
  that can scale. A brand built for one usually loses the other.
approach: >-
  Build the brand on plain, confident proof: field results when they exist,
  clear explanations until they do. One visual system, two front doors on the
  website, each speaking to its audience in its own terms.
developed:
  - Positioning and messaging for farmers and for investors
  - Brand identity, colour, typography and photography direction
  - Website with separate journeys for growers and investors
  - Investor deck and sales sheet templates
  - Content plan for publishing trial updates as they happen
map:
  outside:
    - Identity, website and sales materials
    - Grower and investor journeys
    - Trial update content
  inside:
    - Messaging framework for each audience
    - Templates the team edits themselves
    - Enquiry tracking by audience type
objectives:
  - Look credible to investors at the first meeting
  - Explain the product in one sentence a farmer would repeat
  - Generate qualified enquiries from the website
seoDescription: Concept project. Brand identity and website for an agritech startup speaking to farmers and investors.
---

Startups often over-design. This concept keeps the brand calm and specific, so
the science and the people behind it carry the weight.
